How It Began

A kitchen.
A dream. A bar.

It started with a single dark chocolate bar made in a small Kothrud kitchen in 2020. Founder Arjun Desai had just returned from a chocolate pilgrimage across Europe — Geneva, Brussels, Lyon — and couldn't find anything back home that matched what he'd tasted.

So he made it himself. He spent months learning tempering, ganache ratios, and couverture techniques before gifting his first box to friends at Diwali. Within 48 hours, he had 50 orders. DarkEmber was born.

Today, from our Kothrud atelier, we craft 50+ varieties of premium chocolates — each one a story of obsession, restraint, and love.

What Drives Us

Our Values

🫘
Bean to Bar Purity
We trace every cocoa bean to its origin — Ecuador, Madagascar, Ghana. The terroir of the source expresses itself in every bite, and we never compromise on it.
🤲
Handcrafted Always
Not one of our chocolates has ever touched a production line. Every truffle is hand-rolled, every bar manually tempered, every gift box packed by human hands with care.
✨
Luxury Without Compromise
We believe luxury is not about price — it's about experience. From the first unwrap to the last bite, every detail is designed to feel extraordinary.
🌱
Sustainable Sourcing
We work directly with ethical cocoa cooperatives. No exploitative supply chains. Every purchase supports farmers who are paid fairly for the world's finest crop.
🎁
The Art of Gifting
We believe a gift should feel like a moment, not a transaction. Our packaging, presentation, and personalisation make every DarkEmber gift unforgettable.
🍫
Joy in Every Bite
Above all, we make chocolate that makes people happy. Every recipe starts with one question: does this bring joy? If yes, it earns a place in our collection.
