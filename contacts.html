Need Help?

Common Questions

How long does delivery take? +
Pune deliveries are available same day or next day. Maharashtra orders take 2-3 business days. Pan-India shipping takes 3-5 business days. Express options are available on request.
Do you offer custom corporate gifting? +
Absolutely. We offer fully customised corporate hampers with branded packaging, personalised message cards, and company logo embossing. Minimum order is 10 units. Email corporate@chocohunt.in for a quote.
Can I visit the atelier in Kothrud? +
Yes! Atelier visits are available by appointment Monday to Saturday. Email hello@chocohunt.in or call us to schedule. You'll get to taste our latest creations and watch our chocolatiers at work.
Do you ship chocolates safely in summer? +
All our chocolates are packed in temperature-controlled, insulated packaging. During peak summer months (April–June) we use ice gel packs and recommend next-day delivery for orders within India.
What is your return or refund policy? +
We accept returns only for damaged or incorrect items. Claims must be raised within 24 hours of delivery with photo evidence. Approved refunds are processed within 5-7 business days. Email hello@chocohunt.in.
How can I become a retail partner? +
Visit our Partners page and fill the partnership enquiry form, or email partners@chocohunt.in with your business details. Our team will get back within 2 business days to discuss terms and options.
Are your chocolates vegetarian / vegan? +
Most of our dark chocolate range is vegetarian and many bars are vegan-friendly. Our milk chocolate and cream-filled varieties contain dairy. Each product is clearly labelled. Contact us for specific dietary queries.
